# SignalScout

**Intelligent Lead Qualification & CRM Automation**

Status: **Project 02 — Planned**

SignalScout will demonstrate multi-system automation around lead intake, validation, enrichment, AI-assisted qualification, deterministic scoring rules, CRM-style routing, notifications, and human review for ambiguous prospects.

## Intended Coverage

- lead-form webhooks
- external enrichment APIs or free/mock equivalents
- structured lead scoring
- business-rule routing
- CRM-style persistence
- human review for medium-confidence cases
- audit logs and workflow metrics
- free-first deployment

Development begins only after RelayDesk is complete and measurable.
